Yaydo privacy policy

Your habits stay on your phone. Yaydo is a habit tracker that works on the phone alone. This page says what that means for your data. It is the same policy as in the app, under Settings › Help and about › Privacy policy.

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Contact: theprocolabs@gmail.com

No account, no servers

There is no sign-up and no login. Yaydo has no server of its own, so your habits are never sent to us. We cannot see them, and nobody else can see them through us.

Nothing is tracked

No analytics, no ads and no tracking. Yaydo does not need the internet for anything it does.

Checking for updates

Now and then Yaydo asks if a newer version is out: it reads a small version file, and on iPhone the App Store listing. The request carries nothing about you or your habits, only what any web request does, such as your internet address. Without internet it simply skips the check.

What is kept, and where

Your habits, ticks, notes and settings are kept in Yaydo’s own storage on this phone.

The home-screen widgets read a small copy of today’s habits from the phone too. With App lock on, the Tick today widget hides habit names.

Your phone’s own backup

Android can include Yaydo in its Google backup, and an iPhone in its iCloud backup. That backup belongs to your phone and your Google or Apple account; Yaydo has no login and no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

Backup files

Save a copy makes one small file with your habits, history, notes and settings. It goes only where you choose in the share sheet: Drive, Files, email or a chat. Anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere private.

Restoring a file replaces what is on this phone. App lock stays as it is on this phone.

Reminders

Reminders and nudges are scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing is sent from a server, and they stop when you turn them off.

App lock

App lock uses your phone’s own fingerprint, face or PIN screen. Yaydo only learns whether unlocking worked; it never sees your fingerprint, your face or your PIN.

While App lock is on, Android blocks screenshots of Yaydo and keeps it blank in recent apps, and an iPhone blurs it in the app switcher.

Progress cards

Share cards are drawn on your phone. A card leaves the phone only when you share it, and Save to photos only adds the picture to your photos. Yaydo’s own copy is deleted straight after.

Permissions

Notifications, for reminders. Photos (adding only), to save a progress card. Face ID or fingerprint, for App lock. Each is asked for when it is first needed, and you can change it any time in your phone’s settings.

Deleting your data

Delete Yaydo from your phone and everything it kept there is gone. Copies in your phone’s own backup are managed in your Google or iCloud settings. Backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

Questions
